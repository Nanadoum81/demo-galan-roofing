BEFORE YOU REACH OUT

MAKE YOUR FIRST ROOFING CONVERSATION COUNT.

A few practical details help a roofing professional understand what you need and whether an inspection should be prioritized.

Explain the issue

Describe the visible problem, when you first noticed it, and whether water is actively entering. Mention past repairs or recurring problems.

Identify the property

Include the city, approximate roof age if known, and whether the building is residential, rental, multi-family or commercial.

Share safe photos

Photos from the ground or indoors can show stains, missing pieces and general roof shape. Do not climb onto damaged or unfamiliar roofing.

Ask for next steps

Confirm whether an inspection is available, when you can expect a response, and whether the estimate will be itemized in writing.

Need a roof-specific answer? Request an inspection and ask for a written scope before approving work.
