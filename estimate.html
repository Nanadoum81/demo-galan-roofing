WHAT A ROOFING PROPOSAL SHOULD EXPLAIN.

The on-site findings — not any online tool — determine your written estimate. Use the planner below to map your scope before the inspection.

Scope of work

Confirm exactly what is removed, repaired and installed. Ask whether labor, materials, underlayment, flashing, decking allowances and disposal are included.

Roof dimensions and access

The roof surface is not necessarily equal to floor area. Slope, penetrations, height, accessibility and complexity can materially change a project quote.

Materials and substitutions

Request the proposed materials and installation approach. If products change due to availability, make sure changes are documented and approved.

Payment and timing

Ask for the payment schedule, timing assumptions, permit responsibilities and how unexpected damage will be handled. Financing terms are not verified for Galan Roofing.

Need a roof-specific answer? Request an inspection and ask for a written scope before approving work.
